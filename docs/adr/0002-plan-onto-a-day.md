# A todo is planned onto a day, not given a deadline

You choose which open todos you mean to do on a given day. A todo belongs to one project and is planned onto at most one day, or onto none. There is no due date and no overdue state.

Today shows every open todo planned for today or for an earlier day, so unfinished work is still there the next morning. A future day shows only the open todos planned for that exact day. Past days are not a screen. Completing a todo clears its day and leaves it in the project's completed list. Reopening it plans it onto today. The user can set, change, or clear the planned day. Deleting a project deletes its todos.

Rejected: showing every open todo every day, and treating the date as a deadline.
